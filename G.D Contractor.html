How we work

From first visit to handover

You know what is happening at each stage, what it costs, and what comes next.

  1. STAGE 1

    Site visit

    We see the plot or the rooms and note your needs and budget.

  2. STAGE 2

    Plan and design

    Layout, elevation and interior drawings you can review.

  3. STAGE 3

    Quotation

    An itemised bill of quantities with materials and rates listed.

  4. STAGE 4

    Build

    Structure first, then services, then finishing, with regular site checks.

  5. STAGE 5

    Interiors

    Carpentry, ceilings, paint and fittings installed room by room.

  6. STAGE 6

    Handover

    Final walk-through, snag list cleared, keys in your hand.
